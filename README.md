## The line

> After 214 rejections, the librarian finally shelved her own novel at eye level — and the boy who'd laughed at her drafts last winter asked if he could borrow it first.

167 characters. A long effort finally landing: 214 rejections behind her, the novel shelved at eye level, and the harshest critic from last winter now first in line to read it.
